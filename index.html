Copy, download and submit your conversation

Follow every step. The page removes repeated questions, answers, timestamps and connection text before the PDF is created.

  1. Finish the interview. Ask at least 10 questions.
  2. Copy the conversation. In the chat, mark the conversation from the first message to the final answer. Press Ctrl + C.
  3. Paste it below. Click Paste conversation. If your browser blocks the button, click in the large box and press Ctrl + V.
  4. Check the text. Click Clean conversation. Make sure each question and answer appears once.
  5. Write your name. Use your first name and last initial if that is what your teacher has requested.
  6. Create the PDF. Click Download as PDF. In the print window, choose Save as PDF / Gem som PDF, then click Save / Gem.
  7. Submit the file. Open the assignment in your learning platform, choose Attach/Upload file, select the PDF from Downloads/Overførsler, and finish by clicking Submit/Hand in/Aflever.
